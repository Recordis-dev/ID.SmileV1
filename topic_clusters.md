# AI-SEO Topic Clusters for ID Smile

## Pillar 1: Ortodoncia y Alineación (Orthodontics & Alignment)
*   **Focus:** Core specialty. Correction of bites and teeth alignment.
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-ortodoncia.html`
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-odontopediatria.html` (Cross-link: interceptive orthodontics for kids)

## Pillar 2: Restauración y Rehabilitación (Restoration & Prosthetics)
*   **Focus:** Replacing missing teeth and restoring function.
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-implantes.html` (Implants & Prosthetics)
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-endodoncia.html` (Root canals, saving the natural tooth before restoration)

## Pillar 3: Salud Bucal y Cirugía (Oral Health & Surgery)
*   **Focus:** Foundational health, extractions, and gum care.
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-integral.html` (Comprehensive/General Dentistry, checkups, cleanings)
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-periodoncia.html` (Gum disease treatment)
*   **Target SLP:** `servicio-cirugia.html` (Wisdom teeth, extractions)

## Navigation Strategy
1.  **Top Menu (Mega Menu):** We will replace the current anchor links (`#servicios`, etc.) with a CSS-based dropdown. 
    *   *Tratamientos (Dropdown)* -> Lists the 3 pillars with their respective SLPs.
    *   *Niños* -> Direct link to `servicio-odontopediatria.html`.
    *   *Clínica* -> `index.html#doctores`
    *   *Blog* -> `blog.html`
2.  **Breadcrumbs (in every SLP):** `Inicio > Tratamientos > [Pillar] > [Service]`
3.  **Related Services (Cross-linking at the bottom of SLPs):** E.g., Endodontics links to Implants (if the tooth can't be saved) and Integral (for checkups).
